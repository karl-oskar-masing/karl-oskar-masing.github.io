Kompass privacy policy

Kompass is a personal tool used only by its owner, Karl-Oskar Masing, for his own accounts.

  • What it reads. With the owner's sign-in, Kompass reads his Gmail read-only (gmail.readonly): senders, recipients, subjects, dates and Gmail's short preview of each message. It does not read full message bodies or attachments, and it never sends, changes or deletes mail.
  • Where it is kept. Only on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's keyring. Message details are kept for at most 90 days.
  • Who it is shared with. No one. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party. Nothing is passed to an AI service unless the owner asks for it himself.
  • Google user data. Kompass's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
